A message from Ivan Villamante, Team Lead II for Service Delivery

My role is to help each member of the team succeed—through clear direction, practical coaching, fair accountability, and support when challenges arise.

Let’s keep showing up with professionalism, consistency, and care. When one person grows, the whole team becomes stronger.
